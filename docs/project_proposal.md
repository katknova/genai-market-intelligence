# AI Market Intelligence Dashboard

## 1. Project Background
Generative AI industry has exploded over the past few years, taking by storm not only the tech world, but *every aspect* of people's lives. Public discussion is polarised and heated, with attention often concentrated on a small number of companies and products. Meanwhile news coverage, infrastructure investment, and technological development continue to evolve rapidly.

Businesses, investors, analysts and consumers are exposed to thousands of AI-related articles, product announcements and online discussions every week. Extracting meaningful insight from this volume of information *manually* is increasingly difficult.

This project aims to build an automated market intelligence pipeline that collects AI-related news, analyses public sentiment and discussion trends, and presents the findings through an interactive dashboard and accompanying research report.

## 2. Business Problem
Organisations and individuals struggle to keep-up with and understand how public discussion reflects broader developments within the Generative AI ecosystem.

## 3. Project Objectives
**Business Objectives**
- Measure public sentiment toward the AI ecosystem.
- Identify discussion trends over time.
- Compare sentiment across different AI ecosystem categories.
- Discover patterns worthy of further investigation.
**Technical Objectives**
- Build an automated data pipeline.
- Apply NLP techniques to analyse text.
- Store and clean structured datasets.
- Create an interactive dashboard.
- Produce a professional research report.
## 4. Research Questions
**_Primary_ Question**
*What does public sentiment reveal about the Generative AI ecosystem?*
**_Supporting_ Questions**
- *Which companies receive the greatest share of public attention?*
- *Which AI ecosystem categories receive the most positive and negative sentiment?*
- *How does sentiment change over time?*
- *Which topics dominate AI-related discussions?*
- *Are there notable differences in discussion volume and sentiment between foundation model companies, infrastructure providers and semiconductor manufacturers?*
